## Direction contract
THESIS: The page is the visitor's own spreadsheet, and scrolling turns it into one system. Refuses describing "fragmentation" in prose: it shows it, and shows it resolved.
OWN-WORLD: A spreadsheet app: formula bar with name box and fx, column letters, row numbers, gridlines, amber header fill, highlighted and error cells, red comment marks; then a clean frappe-ui list with status badges. Sheet tabs are the page navigation; the status bar shows Count and Sum of real numbers.
STORY: Recognise the mess (four spellings of "yes", a duplicate, a #VALUE!, a total that says 3), watch the form, chat and script merge into one source and the rows become one list that says 4, then learn what changes, what we build, who we worked with, the stack, and training; then tell us the problem.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Title bar with the file name and the primary action; formula bar showing the A1 formula; merged A1:G5 cell with the selected-cell outline holding the H1 and lede; "Visited this month 3" with a comment; the messy register; a Google Form card, a WhatsApp exchange and a terminal error floating over it; a caption pill; sheet tabs fixed at the bottom.
FORM: Sheet to System, the user's second pick of four. seed: none (unavailable). Signature interaction: a pinned, scroll-scrubbed transformation from sheet to system; the formula bar reads the cell under the pointer; the partners list sorts by column.
